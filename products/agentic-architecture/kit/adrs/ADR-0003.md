# ADR-0003: Enforce Tenant-Scoped Retrieval and Claim-to-Source Attribution

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-24
- **Pattern:** PAT-03
- **Mitigates:** FM-03

## Context
Shared vector indexes and ungrounded synthesis risk leaking cross-tenant context or stating fabricated policy claims.

## Decision
Require a verified tenantId predicate on every retrieval call, wrap retrieved passages in <untrusted-content>, and withhold any generated sentence lacking a valid source marker.

## Alternatives Considered and Rejected
1. **Rejected option 1:** Post-retrieval filtering in prompt text.
2. **Rejected option 2:** shipping ungrounded prose without citation checks.

## Consequences
- **Positive:** Deterministically prevents `FM-03` and produces a machine-checkable audit trail.
- **Negative / Cost:** Adds explicit schema and gate checks to the build pipeline.

## Measurement

Primary measure: intended readers who produce a usable, owned proof artifact that survives independent review. Record numerator, denominator, time window, and definition of usable. Do not count downloads as completed outcomes.

Supporting measures: first-use completion time, accepted-outcome cost, total human review effort, export/restore success, recovery success, unresolved critical defects, and corrections. Establish a baseline before setting commercial targets. Global-readiness checks include editable language, timezone-neutral dates, currency-explicit examples, accessible instructions, and a documented support boundary. These checks do not establish worldwide legal or operational readiness.

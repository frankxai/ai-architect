# Architecture for agency and ownership

Status: proposed verbal and product contract, 2026-09-05. This extends the [authority system](authority-system.md); it does not replace the technical guide or approve a public rebrand.

## Verbal kernel

Frank's north star is **AI architect for a more free world**. Here, freedom means practical agency: choosing what to build, understanding the system, owning the relevant assets, and being able to change direction. It is not a promise of effortless income or freedom from responsibility.

Proposed studio promise:

> We build AI tools, templates, and practical methods that help people build businesses they own, on terms they choose.

Proposed public headline: **Build a business you own. Design a way of working that fits your life.**

For organizations: **Build intelligent systems your people can understand, operate, and change.**

FrankX is the studio and public entry point. Frank remains accountable for judgment and editorial claims. The technical guide keeps its neutral voice and senior technical reader; it must not become a motivational manifesto. The first-person book carries personal conviction, with lived experience included only when Frank supplies or approves the evidence.

Two proposed entry paths serve different jobs:

- Build your freedom: independent builders turning a useful capability into a customer-owned business asset.
- Build intelligent organizations: teams proving one bounded workflow with accountable ownership and operating controls.

The initial organization pilot should be one small-team workflow. This is a working scope choice, not a claim that enterprise work is out of scope. English-first, localization-ready material is the default; global reach is a design requirement, not a verified result.

## Values as requirements

| Value | Required behavior | Evidence | Refuse |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agency | Explain decisions; allow approval, correction, and stopping | A reader can reject an action and recover | Hidden consequential actions |
| Ownership | Document export, licenses, accounts, and replacement seams | An export and restore exercise | Calling a subscription ownership |
| Integrity | Separate observation, inference, prescription, and sales copy | Claim records and correction history | Invented success, scarcity, or endorsement |
| Creativity | Leave room for customer voice and meaningful alternatives | Two viable approaches and editable source | One imposed business or lifestyle template |
| Excellence | Test the actual first-use path and failure paths | Reproducible lab plus independent findings | Self-awarded quality labels |
| Community | Make contribution, credit, consent, and support explicit | Contribution terms and a support owner | Extracting private work for public proof |
| Evolution | Version decisions and revisit them when evidence changes | Review date, changelog, and migration note | Permanent claims about changing technology |

These requirements can conflict. Exportability may cost more; deeper automation can increase review burden. Record the tradeoff in the [agency review](../labs/agency-and-ownership-review.md), rather than claiming all values improve at once.

## Human operating practice

The shared operator core connects values, decisions, behavior, and sustainable capacity. Reality Architect is the proposed method home; Human OS is the proposed product expression. The guide imports the relevant practice, not a second identity or personal-memory standard. Existing reality.md conventions must be inspected before any downstream implementation.

For each work cycle:

1. Choose one outcome and one person it helps.
2. State the value and constraint the work must respect.
3. Identify the current bottleneck before adding automation.
4. Define a small reversible test and its stop condition.
5. Reserve time for review, recovery, and obligations outside work.
6. Compare the observed result with the prediction; change the next action.

Use mental models as working questions: What is reversible? Where is the constraint? What would disprove this? What does this choice make harder next month? Do not frame these prompts as medical treatment or guaranteed peak performance. Personal energy and relationship notes stay private by default and are not product analytics.

## Product boundaries

The guide teaches technical decisions. The personal book explains Frank's judgment. The monthly briefing records meaningful changes. The annual package freezes accepted evidence. Foundry templates should turn these decisions into a business workflow, while Human OS supports the operator's choices and review habits. These are distinct jobs with shared principles, not six competing introductions.

Open core, paid depth is the proposed business model. Keep educational decisions inspectable; charge only for tested depth, maintained implementation, or support with clear scope. Do not change the existing repository license or promise a content license before the rights review. Unlaunched products capture per-product demand; no checkout until the estate release gate passes.

## Buyer research and proof target

Observed reference, checked 2026-09-05: [The Full Stack LLM Bootcamp](https://fullstackdeeplearning.com/llm-bootcamp/) offers practical LLM application education. Its [parent curriculum](https://fullstackdeeplearning.com/) addresses the AI-product lifecycle from problem definition to deployment and user experience.

The product to beat for this specific lab is the Full Stack LLM Bootcamp's practical learning experience. That is a benchmark, not a superiority claim. Our proposed distinction is an explicit ownership, exit, and operator-load proof attached to an architecture decision. We have not established that competing courses lack those concerns.

Buyer hypothesis: a technical founder values a reusable decision record more than another broad introduction. Test with five consenting intended readers. Ask each to complete the lab without estate context, identify the next action, and explain the exit plan. Record confusion and elapsed effort. Five readers give directional learning, not market validation. No customer research has yet been performed for this extension.
